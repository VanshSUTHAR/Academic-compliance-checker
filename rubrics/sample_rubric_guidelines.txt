COURSE SUBMISSION RUBRIC GUIDELINES (CS-401 ADVANCED RESEARCH SEMINAR)

1. General Formatting:
- Body text must be written in 12pt Times New Roman font.
- Margins must be exactly 1.0 inch (2.54 cm) on all four sides.
- Double line spacing (2.0) must be applied throughout the entire document.
- Submission word count must be between 2000 and 3500 words.
- Expected page count is 4 to 8 pages.

2. Document Structure:
- The paper must strictly include the following sections in sequence:
  1. Title Page / Header
  2. Abstract (at least 150 words)
  3. Introduction
  4. Methodology
  5. Results
  6. Discussion
  7. Conclusion
  8. References

3. Referencing and Citations:
- All sources must strictly follow APA 7th Edition citation formatting.
- Every in-text citation (e.g. Author, Year) must correspond to an entry in the References section.
- Minimum of 4 peer-reviewed sources in the bibliography.

4. Identification and Metadata:
- The title page or document header must clearly display:
  - Student Full Name
  - Student Roll / ID Number (e.g., STU-2024-9876)
  - Course Code (CS-401 or CS401)
  - Date of Submission

5. Writing Quality:
- Avoid conversational language, vague terminology (e.g., 'stuff', 'things', 'obviously', 'a lot of').
- Limit excessive run-on sentences (maximum 35 words per sentence).
